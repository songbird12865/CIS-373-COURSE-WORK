Hey Kids! God thinks you are SPECIAL!!!

Jesus Loves Me — Still True

If you ever went to Sunday School, you've heard it: "Jesus loves me, this I know." It's the first song many of us ever learned about faith. And here's the thing — it's still completely true. Whether you're 8, 16, or 25, you are deeply, specifically, personally loved by God. That love didn't expire when you got older or more complicated. If anything, it goes deeper as you come to understand more of what it cost Him.

"For I know the plans I have for you, declares the Lord, plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future."
— Jeremiah 29:11

Take Your Place

In older times, young people were told to "know their place" — to be quiet, to wait, to defer to their elders in everything. But God's message to young people is radically different. He calls young people to lead, to speak, to step up. David was a teenager when he was anointed king. Jeremiah was called as a youth. Mary was a young woman when she was chosen to carry the Son of God. God does not sideline the young — He invites them to the front.

You Are Worth Protecting

Growing up today is genuinely hard. Social media, comparison culture, pressure to perform, questions about identity and belonging — the weight that young people carry is real. God sees every bit of it. He is not sitting in heaven disappointed in your generation. He is fiercely protective of you, proud of you, and calling you by name into something greater than anything the world has promised you. You matter to Him — not someday, but right now, exactly as you are.
