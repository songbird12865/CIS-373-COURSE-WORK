About

A Closer Walk was created by Melissa Joyce and is intended to be a helpful resource to encourage and strengthen others in their faith. If you have been wondering about God and your place in His realm, we hope this website inspires you to begin a journey on the path towards knowing God, "really" knowing God, in a personal and transformative way. Please allow us to share in your journey.

We want to help address your questions like: "Is God really real?" "Does he care about me?" "Why can't I sense Him?" "Is it really possible to know God, personally?" To help answer these questions, there are links to the Bible, Videos, and testimonials from "real" people who can testify to the existence of a Loving and Powerful God. There is also a Contact page where you may send questions or prayer requests. We are here for YOU!
