About God

Who is He, and who is He to you?

"In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth." — Genesis 1:1
Back to Portfolio

God the Creator

Before anything existed — before the stars, before the earth, before time itself — God was. He spoke the universe into existence with nothing more than His word. Every mountain, every ocean, every living creature reflects His majesty and His power. You are not an accident. You are a creation, crafted intentionally by a God who designed you with purpose and breathed life into you.

God the Provider

God doesn't just create and walk away. He sustains everything He has made. From the air we breathe to the food on our tables, every good thing flows from His generous hand. Jesus himself reminded us that God feeds the birds of the air and clothes the wildflowers — how much more does He care for you? Trusting God as your Provider means releasing the weight of worry and leaning into His faithfulness.

God Who Watches Over You

Not a single moment of your life has gone unnoticed by God. The Bible tells us He knows the number of hairs on your head. When you feel invisible, unseen, or forgotten — God sees you. He saw Hagar alone in the desert. He saw Joseph in prison. He saw you in your hardest moment. His eyes are always on those He loves, and that includes you.
