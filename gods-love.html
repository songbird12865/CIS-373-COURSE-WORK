God's Love

Deep. Wide. Unconditional. And it's all for YOU.

"For God so loved the world that He gave His one and only Son." — John 3:16

Does God Still Love Me?

Most of us have asked this question at some point. Maybe you've made mistakes you're not proud of. Maybe life has felt so hard that you've wondered if God even notices. The answer — straight from scripture — is an unshakeable YES. God's love for you is not based on your performance. It is not conditional on you getting everything right. His love is rooted in who He is, not in what you do.

"Neither death nor life, neither angels nor demons, neither the present nor the future, nor any powers, neither height nor depth, nor anything else in all creation, will be able to separate us from the love of God."
— Romans 8:38-39

How Deep Is His Love?

The Apostle Paul prayed that we would have the power to grasp "how wide and long and high and deep is the love of Christ." It surpasses knowledge — meaning it is beyond what our human minds can fully comprehend. God's love reached all the way down to the cross, bearing the weight of everything we've ever done wrong, so that nothing could stand between you and Him.

His Love Is Personal

God's love isn't a general, vague feeling toward humanity as a whole. It is intensely personal. He knows your name. He knows your story. He knows your fears, your dreams, your failures, and your potential. The same God who holds galaxies in place knows the deepest corners of your heart — and loves you still. That is the kind of love the Bible calls "agape" — a selfless, unconditional, sacrificial love that never gives up on you.

The Dimensions of God's Love

Wide

Redeems our entire life - beginning to end. His blood completely covers you

Long

His love spans from Eternity Past through Eternity Future. You were forgiven before you were born

High

He has elevated us to a position of family: Sons and Daughters, to be exact!

Deep

His love goes deeper than our deepest wounds. You cannot possibly go so far that God cannot redeem you!
